## What this project is

**Epistemomechanics is a new scientific field.** This repository is where that field is
developed and where the scientific-visualization and simulation applications that produce and
express its domain knowledge are built. The applications are instruments; the field is the
point.

It is a field **in its own right** — it *adds* domain knowledge. It is **not**:

- a study of other studies,
- a meta-analysis or review,
- a visualization wrapper around someone else's results.

## The field's substance is operator-defined

What epistemomechanics *is* — its objects of study, its laws, its methods, its claims — is
established by the operator (Liz) over time and **documented as it develops** (documents-as-we-go).

> **For agents:** do **not** fabricate the field's doctrine, coin its terminology, or assert
> its results. PROBE what has actually been written (here, in `research/`, in loom-memory) and
> treat anything unstated as open. When the operator defines a piece of the field, record it;
> do not pre-fill it. This is a research project: being wrong-but-confident about a new field
> is worse than leaving a gap visible.
